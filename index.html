Data Analysis & Predictive Modeling

Apartments in Almaty

The dataset comprises approximately 12,000 real estate listings across Almaty, documenting crucial attributes including administrative district, total area, floor level, building construction type, year built, and listing price. It serves as an optimal foundation for predictive regression modeling and spatial price distribution analysis.

View Dataset on Kaggle

Project Objectives

  • 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Evaluate price per square meter across administrative districts, building materials, and construction periods.
  • 2. Data Cleaning & Outlier Removal: Filter out duplicate listings, corrupt floor numbers, and extreme pricing outliers.
  • 3. Predictive Valuation Modeling: Train machine learning algorithms (Random Forest, LightGBM) to forecast market apartment prices.

Statistical Hypothesis Testing

1. One-Way ANOVA: District Price Disparity

Null Hypothesis (H₀): Mean apartment price per square meter is equal across all administrative districts of Almaty.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁): At least one district has a significantly different mean price per square meter.

Test Result: p-value < 0.001 (Reject H₀). There is a statistically significant price premium in Medeu and Bostandyk districts.

2. Two-Sample T-Test: Monolithic vs. Panel Buildings

Null Hypothesis (H₀): There is no difference in the average price per square meter between monolithic and panel buildings.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁): Monolithic buildings cost significantly more per square meter than panel buildings.

Test Result: p-value < 0.01 (Reject H₀). Monolithic structures carry an average validated premium of 22% over panel units.

3. Shapiro-Wilk Test: Price Normality

Null Hypothesis (H₀): Real estate prices follow a normal distribution.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁): Real estate prices deviate from a normal distribution.

Test Result: p-value < 0.001 (Reject H₀). The price distribution is strongly right-skewed; logarithmic transformation is required for linear modeling.

Key Findings

  1. Location is Primary: Medeu and Bostandyk properties trade at a 35% to 50% premium relative to Turksib and Alatau districts.
  2. Structural Longevity: Modern monolithic construction built after 2010 demonstrates significantly higher value retention than Soviet housing stock.
  3. Predictive Baseline: An ensemble Gradient Boosting Regressor achieved an R² score of ~0.82 on the test split.
